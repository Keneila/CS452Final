##Initial Design
My final project is going to be a menstrual cycle manager. I want to provide a site that saves your menstrual cycle pattern, your health habits, and your symptoms, and gives you a prediction of both your menstrual phase is, but also when your other phases are and what symptoms are most likely for you at any time of the month based on your habits.
